## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-integrity clinical environment engineered for hospital governance, incident reporting, risk grading, and quality compliance. The design language must project absolute precision, calm authority, and rapid legibility under high-cognitive-load situations (such as sentinel events, mortality reviews, and active compliance audits).

The visual aesthetic combines **Corporate Modernism** with crisp clinical utility:
- **Tone:** Uncompromisingly clinical, objective, analytical, and reassuringly stable.
- **Visual Tension:** Cool slate surfaces balanced against high-impact clinical risk signals.
- **Structure:** Crisp, hair-line framing, dense tabular structures, deliberate data hierarchy, and intentional absence of decorative distractions or playful embellishments.
- **Cognitive Ergonomics:** High visual contrast ratios, distinct status identification without relying exclusively on color hue, and zero visual ambiguity for mission-critical medical governance.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around healthcare safety protocols, regulatory reporting standards, and clinical risk matrices.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#047857` - Emerald 700):** Signifies clinical safety, resolution, certified accreditation, and verified protocol adherence.
- **Secondary (`#475569` - Slate 600):** Establishes institutional balance, structural typography, and technical metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#0284c7` - Sky 600):** Applied sparingly for informational states, interactive tooltips, audit logs, and external reference links.
- **Neutral Base:** Anchored by Cool Slate (`#0f172a` Slate 900 for high-contrast typography, `#f8fafc` Slate 50 for page canvases, and `#f1f5f9` Slate 100 for secondary containers).

### Clinical Risk Grading Matrix (Incident Severity Classification)
Clinical governance requires standardized triage and risk evaluation coding:
- **Extreme Risk / Merah (Score 15–25):** `#e11d48` (Rose 600) with `#fff1f2` (Rose 50) surface tint and `#9f1239` (Rose 800) text. Triggers immediate root-cause investigation within 45 days and mandatory board notification.
- **High Risk / Jingga (Score 10–14):** `#f97316` (Orange 500) with `#fff7ed` (Orange 50) surface tint and `#9a3412` (Orange 800) text. Triggers clinical head review and detailed risk assessment within 2 weeks.
- **Moderate Risk / Kuning (Score 4–9):** `#eab308` (Yellow 500) with `#fefce8` (Yellow 50) surface tint and `#854d0e` (Yellow 800) text. Standard clinical investigation within 45 days.
- **Low Risk / Hijau (Score 1–3):** `#059669` (Emerald 600) with `#ecfdf5` (Emerald 50) surface tint and `#065f46` (Emerald 800) text. Routine monitoring and department-level resolution.

### Surface and Outline Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#f8fafc` (Slate 50)
- **Surface Elevation 0 (Card/Panel):** `#ffffff` (White)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Hover/Secondary Panel):** `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100)
- **Structural Outlines:** `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200) for standard layout grids; `#cbd5e1` (Slate 300) for active form fields and table inner borders.

## Typography

The type system utilizes `Inter` exclusively to maintain systematic legibility, clean horizontal metrics, and high x-height readability in dense tabular layouts.

### Usage Principles
- **Tabular Numerics:** All medical record numbers, incident counts, risk scores, and temporal markers must enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent optical jitter during row sorting and live polling.
- **Hierarchy Separation:** Section titles and risk card headings use semibold and bold weights to provide immediate orientation without competing with key metrics.
- **Labels & Tags:** Risk level badges, medical unit designations, and validation tags use uppercase or semi-bold micro-labels with slight letter-spacing to enhance legibility at small viewport scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a **12-column fluid grid** for desktop audit dashboards and transforms into unified single-column card flows for mobile inspection workflows.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin. Maximum canvas width is bounded at 1600px to maintain eye-tracking control across wide clinical data sheets.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) gutter, 24px (`1.5rem`) margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) gutter, 16px (`1rem`) margin. Clinical cards and severity indicators reflow into full-width stacking.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Field & Cell Density:** Data tables maintain dense vertical spacing using `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments to maximize above-the-fold visibility of critical alerts.
- **Card Separations:** Dashboard metric cards and clinical analytical blocks standardize on `space-md` gaps internally and `space-lg` separations between functional modules.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain high visual acuity and sterile clinical integrity, this design system avoids soft, floating skeuomorphism, noisy blurs, and deep decorative shadows. Visual hierarchy is established via **crisp borders paired with subtle tonal contrast**.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (App Shell Canvas):** `#f8fafc` (Slate 50). Provides a calm backdrop that eliminates screen glare on clinical monitors.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `#ffffff` (White), enclosed with a 1px solid `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200) structural border. No ambient blur shadow.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Panels & Risk Modals):** `#ffffff` enclosed with a 1px solid `#cbd5e1` (Slate 300) border and an intentional, low-spread ambient shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Alert Drawers & Floating Context Sheets):** `#ffffff` bordered with `#94a3b8` (Slate 400) and elevated by `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Active Focus Rings:** Standardized 2px offset ring with 2px width in Primary Emerald `#047857` (or corresponding risk color when editing incident fields).

## Shapes

The shape architecture is calibrated to **Soft (Level 1)**. This ensures that buttons, tables, badges, and card panels present clean, engineered geometry without harsh brutalist corners or casual, overly rounded curves.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Table Rows, Dropdowns):** `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Surface Containers (Cards, Modals, Analytic Modules):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Clinical Badges & Risk Pills:** `0.25rem` (4px) to retain an authoritative, stamp-like appearance. Full pills (`9999px`) are strictly prohibited in clinical matrices to avoid conflation with casual mobile tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#047857` (Emerald 700) background, `#ffffff` text, 1px solid `#047857`. Hover state: `#065f46` (Emerald 800). Active state: `#064e3b` (Emerald 900). Height: 36px (dense) or 40px (default).
- **Secondary:** `#ffffff` background, `#334155` (Slate 700) text, 1px solid `#cbd5e1` (Slate 300). Hover state: `#f8fafc` background, `#0f172a` text, `#94a3b8` border.
- **Destructive / High-Alert Action:** Solid `#e11d48` (Rose 600) with `#ffffff` text. Used exclusively for sentinel event declarations and incident sign-offs.

### Clinical Risk Chips & Badges
- **Structure:** 1px solid border matching 30% alpha of the risk grade, with tinted background and dark contrast text.
- **Extreme Risk (Merah 15–25):** Background `#fff1f2`, border `#fecdd3`, text `#9f1239`. Prefix with mandatory numerical score indicator (e.g., `[15-25] EKSTREM`).
- **High Risk (Jingga 10–14):** Background `#fff7ed`, border `#ffedd5`, text `#9a3412`. Prefix with `[10-14] TINGGI`.
- **Moderate Risk (Sedang 4–9):** Background `#fefce8`, border `#fef08a`, text `#854d0e`. Prefix with `[4-9] SEDANG`.
- **Low Risk (Rendah 1–3):** Background `#ecfdf5`, border `#a7f3d0`, text `#065f46`. Prefix with `[1-3] RENDAH`.

### Clinical Governance Data Tables
- **Header:** Background `#f8fafc`, 1px solid bottom border `#cbd5e1`. Text styled in `label-md`, color `#475569`, uppercase.
- **Rows:** Zebra striping alternate `#ffffff` and `#f8fafc`. Cell padding: 10px 16px. Border-bottom: 1px solid `#e2e8f0`. Hover row: `#f1f5f9`.
- **Key Columns:** Incident ID (tabular monospace font), Patient Identifier Hash, Clinical Department, Incident Severity Matrix badge, and Assigned Risk Officer.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs:** Height 38px, background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`, radius 4px. Placeholder: `#94a3b8`. Focus: border `#047857`, ring 2px `#047857` with 20% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square/circle, 1px solid `#94a3b8`. Checked: `#047857` with sharp white interior tick mark.

### Metric Cards
- **Architecture:** White surface, 1px solid `#e2e8f0` border, 16px interior padding.
- **Content Flow:** Small upper tracking label (`label-sm`, `#64748b`), followed by primary value in `metric-display` (`#0f172a`), accompanied by trend indicator pill (emerald for resolved safety margins, rose for adverse event spikes).

### Clinical Bar Charts & Visualization Widgets
- **Grid Lines:** Horizontal rules rendered with `#e2e8f0` stroke, 1px dashed.
- **Bar Marks:** Bars feature 2px top radius; zero horizontal padding between paired comparison bars. Use categorical color assignment directly linked to risk matrices (Rose-600 for sentinel rates, Emerald-600 for protocol compliance).
- **Tooltip Popovers:** Sharp slate container (`#0f172a`), white tabular text, zero blur, 4px border radius.
